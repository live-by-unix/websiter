# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer (live-by-unix) via GitHub. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers have the
right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
